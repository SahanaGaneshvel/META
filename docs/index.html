How a site uses it

You give it your meter data. It tells you how many chargers fit, then runs them.

You give

  • Your building's meter dataone year, downloaded from the utility (a Green Button file)
  • Your utility tariffthe PDF; Muse Spark reads it and checks it reproduces your bill
  • Your grid connection size and chargersfor example 1,000 kW, and how many chargers you want
  • Questions, any timeby text or voice: "6 vans need 40 kWh by 6 am. Can we do it?"

Headroom does

  • Simulates real drivers at your buildingmonths of charging, 15 minutes at a time, before you buy anything
  • Plans every 15 minutes, once installedforecasts the building, charges in the room it leaves
  • Guarantees the limita safety layer nothing can override
  • Sends each charger its powerover OCPP, which commercial chargers already speak

You get

  • How many chargers fit"your connection fits 48, not 36", with the monthly cost
  • Every driver fully charged100% of the energy they asked for, 0 times over the limit
  • A lower bill32% lower demand charges than standard smart charging
  • Checked answers"Yes: 240 kWh needed, 409 kWh deliverable without a new peak."

01 · The problem

Every building wants more chargers. They all share one grid connection.

Owners size chargers for the worst case, the building's peak plus every charger at full power. So they install fewer, or wait for a costly grid upgrade.

02 · The bill

Utilities bill your highest 15 minutes of the month.

What one 50 kW charging spike at 6 pm adds to a summer bill under Palo Alto's E-4 tariff, for $3 of electricity.

How it works

Every 15 minutes, four steps. The AI talks; the math decides.

01

See the building

A forecast of the building's next 24 hours, with a calibrated worst case.

02

Plan the charging

A linear program fills the room under the peak already paid for. Drivers come first.

03

Guarantee the limit

A deterministic safety layer has the last word. Nothing above it can override it.

04

Send to the chargers

Each charger gets its power limit over OCPP, valid for 15 minutes, so it fails safe.

Meta Muse Spark

The site manager just asks: "Can I add 12 more chargers? What would it cost me?" Muse Spark answers by running Headroom's own tools, and every number it says is checked against them. If the internet drops, Muse Glimmer answers from the site's own GPU.
