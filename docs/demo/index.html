1 · The problem

Chargers that can't see the building pile onto its peak.

Standard smart charging splits a fixed charger budget between the cars plugged in. It never sees the building, so on a hot evening it adds its load right on top of the building's peak, the 15 minutes the utility bills.

2 · How Headroom works

Every 15 minutes: see, plan, guarantee, send.

Press play. Headroom forecasts the building, charges in the room under the peak the month has already paid for, checks the grid limit, and sends each charger its power. The bill counters run for the same cars under both approaches.

3 · The AI operator

The site manager just asks. Meta's Muse Spark answers.

Muse Spark can only act through checked tools. It never touches the chargers, and every number it says is traced back to a tool result. These are its real recorded answers. Cut the internet and Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer answers on site.
