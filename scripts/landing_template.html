01 · The problem

Every building wants more chargers. They all share one grid connection.

Owners size chargers for the worst case, the building's peak plus every charger at full power. So they install fewer, or wait for a costly grid upgrade.

02 · The bill

Utilities bill your highest 15 minutes of the month.

03 · What Headroom does

It sees the building and charges in the room it leaves.

A real heat-wave day at a real building. Charging (teal) fills the room under the peak the month has already paid for, re-planned every 15 minutes, while a safety layer guarantees the limit.

04 · Muse Spark runs the site

The site manager just asks.

Meta's Muse Spark answers through checked tools: it never touches the chargers, and every number it says is traced to the math. If the internet drops, Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer takes over on site.
